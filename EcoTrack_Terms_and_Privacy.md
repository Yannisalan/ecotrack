# PART 2: PRIVACY POLICY

## 1. Introduction

EcoTrack respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. It is designed to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and follows good-practice principles such as GDPR where applicable.

## 2. Information We Collect

**a) Information you provide**

- Account details: name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form);
- Profile details (optional): username, country/city, profile settings;
- Activity data: transportation (mode, distance), electricity usage, water usage, waste (type, quantity), and related dates.

**b) Information collected automatically**

- Device and browser type, operating system, IP address;
- Log data such as access times, pages/screens viewed, and error reports;
- Authentication tokens (JWT) used to keep you signed in.

**c) Derived data**

- Calculated CO₂, water, and waste statistics, your Eco Score, and personalised recommendations.

We do **not** collect sensitive personal data such as financial details, government IDs, or health information, and we do not track your precise location unless you explicitly enable a feature that needs it.
